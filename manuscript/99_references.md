# References

1. Buehlmann, A. A. (1984). *Decompression–Decompression Sickness*.
   Berlin: Springer-Verlag. (Source of the ZH-L16 tissue-model family, its
   compartment half-times, and the tolerated-ambient-pressure formulation
   `P_amb_tol = (P_tissue - a) * b` that the M-value line inverts.)
2. Naval Sea Systems Command (2016). *U.S. Navy Diving Manual*, Revision 7
   (SS521-AG-PRO-010). Washington, DC. (Operational reference for air
   no-decompression limits; the published no-stop bands the test suite pins
   this package's limits against.)
3. Urick, R. J. (1983). *Principles of Underwater Sound*, 3rd ed.
   New York: McGraw-Hill. (The passive-sonar equation: transmission loss,
   signal excess, detection threshold.)
4. Thorp, W. H. (1967). Analytic description of the low-frequency attenuation
   coefficient. *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 42(1),
   270. (The frequency-dependent absorption formula used in the sonar model.)
5. Newman, J. N. (1977). *Marine Hydrodynamics*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.
   (Resistance and propulsive power for submerged bodies; the basis of the
   cubic speed–power drag law in the logistics model.)
6. Bowditch, N. (2002). *The American Practical Navigator* (Pub. No. 9).
   Bethesda, MD: National Imagery and Mapping Agency. (Set-and-drift
   navigation: the current vector triangle, course-to-steer, and the
   maneuvering-board relative-motion construction.)
7. Cockcroft, A. N., & Lameijer, J. N. F. (2011). *A Guide to the Collision
   Avoidance Rules*, 7th ed. Oxford: Butterworth-Heinemann. (Relative-motion
   CPA/TCPA geometry and closing-speed practice.)
8. Bond API / THE PROTOCOL — `bond_api` frozen `MissionProvider` protocol,
   BOND suite Layer 1 (in-repo).
9. Fleming, I. (1961). *Thunderball*. London: Jonathan Cape. (Source
   material; the underwater recovery and the *Disco Volante* submersible
   yacht inspire the mission scenario.)
