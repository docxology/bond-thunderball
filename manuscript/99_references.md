# References

1. Buehlmann, A. A. (1984). *Decompression–Decompression Sickness*.
   Berlin: Springer-Verlag. (Descriptive reference for the ZH-L16 tissue
   model family.)
2. Urick, R. J. (1983). *Principles of Underwater Sound*, 3rd ed.
   New York: McGraw-Hill. (The passive-sonar equation: transmission loss,
   signal excess, detection threshold.)
3. Thorp, W. H. (1967). Analytic description of the low-frequency attenuation
   coefficient. *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 42(1),
   270. (The frequency-dependent absorption formula used in the sonar model.)
4. Bowditch, N. (2002). *The American Practical Navigator* (Pub. No. 9).
   Bethesda, MD: National Imagery and Mapping Agency. (Set-and-drift
   navigation: the current vector triangle and course-to-steer.)
5. Bond API / THE PROTOCOL — `bond_api` frozen `MissionProvider` protocol,
   BOND suite Layer 1 (in-repo).
6. Fleming, I. (1961). *Thunderball*. London: Jonathan Cape. (Film source
   material; the underwater recovery and the *Disco Volante* submersible
   yacht inspire the mission scenario.)
